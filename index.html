Questions, answered

Can't find yours? Write to us — a person replies, usually the same day.

Contact us
Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Sketchbook plan is free for good, no card needed. It's plenty for testing whether Formline fits how you already work.

Who owns what I make here?

You do. Full stop. Your sketches, prompts, boards and outputs are yours on every plan, and on paid plans nothing you make is ever used to improve our models.

Can my whole studio share one workspace?

That's the idea. Admins and editors take paid seats; reviewers who only need to look and comment are free, however many you invite.

How does billing work for teams?

One invoice, handled by your admin. All paid seats land in the same billing cycle, and free reviewer seats never touch the bill.

Can I switch from monthly to yearly?

Any time. Your admin flips it in billing settings and the annual rate kicks in at the next cycle.

What file types can I bring in?

Photos, scans of paper sketches, PNG, JPG, layered files, plus OBJ, STL and STEP on the 3D side. If your studio lives in something odd, tell us — we probably want to support it.
